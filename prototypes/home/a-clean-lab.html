Bring your own word lists

Add your entities, protected words, synonyms and a Hunspell dictionary. Search Lens checks each file, saves it in this browser, and uses it when it analyzes your text and your query. Nothing is saved on the cluster.

entity

Entities

Phrases kept as one token. Example: ai supplychain matches only as the exact phrase.

# entity.txt, one phrase per line
ai supplychain
machine learning
new york

keyword_marker

Protected words

Words the stemmer must not change. Example: keep iPhone as it is.

# protected.txt, one word per line
iphone
adidas
running

synonym

Synonyms

Words that mean the same thing. Example: sneakers also finds running shoes.

# synonyms.txt, Solr format
sneakers, running shoes
tv => television
mobile, cell phone

hunspell

Hunspell dictionary

A real dictionary for stemming. Example: ran becomes run.

# two files for one language
en_US.aff   rules
en_US.dic   words

How it works

Your browser never talks to the cluster. The Search Lens backend does it for you.
